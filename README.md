# Pocket Key (data pack, Minecraft Java 26.3)

A portable pocket island you open with a magical key. No mod loader needed.

## Install
Put `pocket-key.zip` into `<your world>/datapacks/`, or pick it under "Data Packs"
when creating a world. The pocket dimension is only created when a world loads, so
after adding it to an existing world, quit to the title screen and reopen the world.

## The key
Every player gets a Pocket Key the first time they join, and a new one every time
they respawn. The key has a hidden Curse of Vanishing, so it disappears when you die
instead of dropping. You never get a second key while you already have one.

## Using it
- Right-click to enter your pocket. You must be at full health and not have taken
  damage for 60 seconds.
- Right-click again inside to go back to exactly where you left from.
  This doesn't work while falling, so the void is a real threat.
- Right-click a mob that a lead can attach to (cows, horses, wolves, allays...) to
  send it into your pocket. Mobs sent in never despawn. Open your pocket once first.

## Effects
- Using the key starts a one second charge-up: a glowing key spins above your head,
  twin helixes spiral around you, rune circles turn on the ground, energy streams and
  enchanting glyphs pull inward, and your screen blurs (`/posteffect`, new in 26.3).
- Then a flash and implosion, and you arrive in a shockwave ring with a light pillar
  and a chime arpeggio. Entering is purple and cyan. Leaving is gold and white.
- Taking damage during the charge-up makes it fizzle out in smoke.
- Inside the pocket, a faint shimmering veil drifts along the invisible walls,
  glowing motes float in the void outside, and portal mist rises from below.
- Mobs you send in vanish in a flash and pop out in a ring on your island.

The animation frames are generated by `tools/gen_fx.py`. Edit that and re-run it
from the pack folder to tweak colours, timings or sounds.

## The pocket
- Starts as an 8x8 grass island floating in the void.
- Hard capped at one chunk (16x16) by an invisible barrier ring and ceiling.
  No world border.
- Fall off and you die in the void with your items.
- No mobs spawn naturally. Beds and respawn anchors don't set your spawn there.
- The chunk is always loaded, so crop and animal farms keep running.
- Every player gets their own pocket the first time they use their key. Pockets sit in
  a row, each one chunk apart, so you can see your friends' islands across the gap.
  Only you can enter yours, and the invisible walls stop anyone crossing over.
